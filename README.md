# Mythic Realms Modpack

Mythic Realms is a single-mod Minecraft adventure that delivers an epic magic system, a god-like boss encounter, a structured quest line, and a living, breathing world—all packaged into one self-contained Fabric mod for Minecraft 1.20.1. With explosive fire magic, chilling frost effects, a towering Verdant Titan boss, an immersive quest book, and ambient world enhancements like drifting embers and autonomous forest regrowth, this mod transforms your gameplay into a mythic journey.

## Install

1. Install the **Fabric Loader** for Minecraft 1.20.1 from [fabricmc.net](https://fabricmc.net/use/).
2. Download and install the **Fabric API** for Minecraft 1.20.1.
3. Place the `verdant-epoch-1.0.0.jar` file into your `mods` folder.
4. Launch Minecraft with the Fabric profile.

**Requirements:** Minecraft 1.20.1, Fabric Loader 0.15.0 or newer, Fabric API, Java 17 or newer.

## Use

### Getting the Items

**Creative Mode:** Open the "Verdant Epoch" creative tab to access the Fire Staff, Frost Wand, and Quest Book directly.

**Survival Crafting:**
- **Fire Staff**: Blaze rod over a stick (B/B/S pattern)
- **Frost Wand**: Two diamonds over a stick (D/D/S pattern)
- **Quest Book**: Paper over string (P/S pattern)

### Epic Magic

- **Fire Staff**: Right-click to launch a volley of 5 explosive fireballs with slight spread. Each shot deals fire damage and has a chance to ignite targets.
- **Frost Wand**: Right-click to release a wave of frost that slows (Slowness IV) and weakens (Weakness II) all living entities within a 3-block radius of your aim point for 10 seconds.

### God-like Boss

**The Verdant Titan:** A colossal boss with:
- 420 health points
- 14 armor points (heavy protection)
- 22 attack damage per hit
- 75% damage reduction from all sources
- Knockback attacks that send players flying
- Random celestial lightning strikes on its target
- Spawns automatically in the Overworld every few minutes, far from players

### Quest Line

**Verdant Epoch Quest Book:** Right-click to open an immersive 4-chapter storyline:
- Chapter I: The Sleeping Epoch — Begin your path as an apprentice mage
- Chapter II: The Living World — Discover the world's ambient wonders
- Chapter III: Awakening the Titan — Learn to track the ancient boss
- Chapter IV: The God's Fall — Complete your journey by defeating the Titan

Navigate chapters with the Prev/Next buttons on-screen.

### Living World Features

- **Night Fireflies**: Drifting ember particles surround players during nighttime
- **Autonomous Forest Growth**: Oak, birch, and spruce saplings occasionally grow on their own, allowing forests to reclaim cleared lands
- **Dynamic Boss Spawning**: The Titan stirs periodically, keeping the world dangerous

## Building from Source

To build the mod from source code:

```bash
./gradlew build
```

The compiled mod will appear in `build/libs/` as `verdant-epoch-1.0.0.jar`.
